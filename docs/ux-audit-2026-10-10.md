# Bait live site: performance and presentation audit (2026-10-10)

Site: https://bait-production-2706.up.railway.app. Read-only pass: no forms submitted, no logins, nothing that raises a dialog. Shots were saved as `docs/ux-audit-2026-10-10-shots/*.png` (not committed).

## Summary

1. The site is fast and clean. Cold LCP runs about 0.4 to 0.6 s on every route, warm TTFB is 50 to 160 ms, CLS is 0, and no route logged a console error or warning.
2. PR 13's claim holds. At 390 px and 820 px, none of the 7 routes overflows the page (`scrollWidth == clientWidth` everywhere). The only sideways scrollers are the intentional movers strip and the category chips.
3. Biggest trust gap: nothing on the site tells a visitor how old the data is. Right now 13 of 16 indexer chains last reported about 20 h ago, yet the UI says only "refreshed nightly" and keeps a green "Live" dot lit whatever the feed status.
4. Biggest presentation bug: at 1280 px the project header's stat tiles cut off the headline numbers ("$8…", "+2…", "$1…"). Next to them, raw week-over-week percentages like +297195% make the feed look broken.
5. Weight: the home and project HTML each inline about 910 KB of RSC data (1,658 project rows) to drive client-side tabs and search. That is 139 KB gzipped, against about 6 KB of visible text.

Shared baseline on every route is about 460 KB: about 280 KB of scripts (the largest are 128 KB and 72 KB), 142 KB of fonts (4 Inter woff2 files: 48 + 48 + 23 + 22 KB) and 36 KB of CSS. No single request was over 300 KB. The only requests over 500 ms were the first-hit trades call, the leaders prefetch and the cold launches API.

Cache headers: `/`, `/leaders`, `/building` and `/week` return `cache-control: private, no-cache, no-store`, so they render on every request. `/about` is prerendered (`x-nextjs-cache: HIT`). Project pages are ISR. `/api/trades` sends `max-age=60, s-maxage=120`.

**How the trades feed loads:** "Live trades" renders server-side as one line, "Loading trades…", under a green live dot. It swaps to the list when `/api/trades` returns (198 ms warm, 1.2 s cold), and the list box is capped at 360 px. Measured CLS was 0. The "Daily volume" bars are server-rendered, so that chart never waits on the API. If upstream fails, the text becomes "Trade feed unavailable, retrying in 60s.", but the green dot stays on.

**Measurement caveats:** The Chrome extension tab reported `visibilityState: hidden`. Its paint timings were therefore invalid (FCP 6.8 s was a background-tab artifact), and window resize and typing did not take effect. The site sends `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, so the in-page iframe viewport trick was blocked. Paint, LCP, CLS, the responsive pass and interaction tests were run in Playwright (Chromium) instead.

## Findings, ranked by impact

### 1. No freshness or staleness signal anywhere (High, small to medium)
- **Route:** every route.
- **Observed:** `/api/health` lists 13 chains as last reported at 2026-10-09 19:33–19:36Z (over 20 h before this test), BNB 152,197 blocks behind, and Base not backfilled. A visitor sees none of this. The footer says "refreshed nightly" with no date, `/leaders` says "refreshed nightly", and `/building` says "Refreshed nightly". Every "Just launched" row is Ethereum (`/api/launches`: 20 of 20 on chain 1) and nothing explains why. The web source has no freshness UI at all (grep for stale/asOf/windowEnd finds only `api/health`). The "Live trades" dot is always green, including over trades 21 h old (VANTRO pool, `k-new-tab.png`) and over the upstream-error state.
- **Why it matters:** Bait ranks by recency. A Trending list built on a window that silently froze for most chains looks just like a live one.
- **Recommendation:** Add one line to the top bar or the list header: "Data through Oct 9 (UTC) · nightly run 06:12". Read it from the receipt `/api/health` already loads (`receipt.day`, `project_daily.window_end`). When health has `reasons`, add an amber chip, "Indexer catching up on 13 chains", plus a per-chain dot in the chain selector. Show the green live dot only when the status is `ok` and the newest trade is under about 15 min old; otherwise show grey "Last trade 21h ago".

### 2. Desktop stat tiles truncate the headline numbers (High, small)
- **Route:** `/` (detail pane) and `/p/[slug]` at desktop widths.
- **Observed:** At a 1280 px viewport, 3 of 6 tiles are cut off. HookrModularHookV6 shows "$8…", "+2…", "$1…"; the measured widths were 45 px visible against 61 / 89 / 61 px needed. Angstrom shows "$1…", "+1…", "$5.…", "9,4…". At 1440 px, "+2971…" is still cut. Phone and tablet use a 2-column grid and show everything.
- **Evidence:** `02-home-stat-tiles-truncated.png`, `01-home-desktop.jpg`, `05-project-angstrom-desktop.jpg`, `k-search-angst.png` (1440).
- **Why it matters:** These are the numbers the card exists to show. A number with an ellipsis reads as broken.
- **Recommendation:** Never ellipsize numerals. Switch the tile row to `grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr)` (two rows) when the centre column is under about 560 px, or use `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(96px, 1fr))`. Shortening labels ("Wallets 24h" → "Wallets") also buys width.

### 3. Raw week-over-week percentages explode (High, small)
- **Route:** `/` list, the movers strip, the detail tiles and the Signals card.
- **Observed:** The feed shows +297195%, +13913% and +7560% in bright green, and the Signals card repeats "297195% week over week". The scoring rule already clamps the ratio to between 0.5 and 10 and uses a $1k floor (DESIGN, Scoring), but the display shows the unclamped number.
- **Why it matters:** These percentages fill the right edge of the feed, the most-scanned column. They tell a visitor nothing beyond "last week was near zero", and they make the data look untrustworthy.
- **Recommendation:** When `vol_7d_prev` is under $1k, show "new" (the VANTRO card already does this) or ">999%". Otherwise round to whole percent. This matches the floor the scorer uses.

### 4. A first-time visitor can tell what a card is but not why it ranks there (Medium, small)
- **Route:** `/`.
- **Observed:** Within 5 s a row reads clearly: rank, name, one-liner or chain, 7d volume, change. But Trending #1 has $80.2M and #13 has $587.7M, #15 Angstrom has $135.5M, and nothing on screen explains the order. The only explanation is on `/about`, and it is not linked from the list.
- **Evidence:** `01-home-desktop.jpg`, `m-820-home.png`.
- **Recommendation:** Put one muted line under the tabs per tab: Trending "Ranked by 7d volume × growth vs last week", New "First seen in the last 7 days", All "By 7d volume". Link each to `/about#scoring`. Add a title or tap hint on the rank numeral.

### 5. Project-page narrative contradicts its own tiles and sits at the bottom (Medium, small to medium)
- **Route:** `/p/angstrom`.
- **Observed:** "Why people care" says "about $119.7M … over 7 days … up from $116.3M", while the header tiles say 7d $135.5M, +16%. On desktop that block is the last panel in the centre column, after Pools, Activity, Signals and Top wallets. On a phone it is about 5 screens down. DESIGN puts "Why people care" in the card itself (Well 2) as the reason to care.
- **Evidence:** `06-project-angstrom-why-people-care.jpg`, `m-390-project-full.png`.
- **Recommendation:** Either regenerate the blurb when 7d volume moves more than about 10%, or keep figures out of the blurb and let the tiles carry the numbers. Move the block directly under the header tiles.

### 6. Trust signals are partly decorative (Medium, small to medium)
- **Route:** `/p/[slug]`.
- **Observed:** The green "Audit linked" badge is not a link, and no audit URL appears among the page's external links. Those links are Uniswap pool links, explorer tx and wallet links, a Blockscout hook link, and Website / GitHub / X. The Exa receipts from DESIGN (two link rows with source and age) do not appear on the project page. Badge explanations are `title` tooltips only, which never show on touch devices. The good part: the hook address links to Blockscout, trades link to Etherscan, and the hooklist check works.
- **Recommendation:** Make "Audit linked" an `<a>` to the audit. Render the 2 receipt rows under "Why people care". Turn badge explanations into a tap-to-expand popover, or a one-line legend under the badges on phones.

### 7. About 910 KB of inline RSC data on home and project pages (Medium, medium)
- **Route:** `/` and `/p/[slug]`.
- **Observed:** 911,303 chars of inline `<script>` on home and 947 KB of decoded HTML on the Angstrom page. The payload has 1,658 rows, each with name, slug, chains, whatLine, color, category, secondaryCategories, firstSeenAt, isNew, isWash, scoreTrending, tvlUsd, routable, audited, hooks and rank. Only 30 rows are visible, and the visible text is about 6 KB. This is what makes tab switching and search instant: typing "angst" filtered to 2 matches with no network call (`k-search-angst.png`). It also fits DESIGN's "no client-side data fetching".
- **Why it matters:** Desktop doesn't notice (LCP about 0.5 s). On a mid-range Android, roughly 1 MB of HTML to parse plus hydration of 1,658 rows is the main cost, and it is paid on every project page too.
- **Recommendation:** Keep the client-side model but trim the row shape to what the list renders (drop secondaryCategories, firstSeenAt, color and hooks, and shorten whatLine to its truncated length). Or ship the top 100 per tab inline and load the full index on first search focus. Either should cut the payload by about half or more.

### 8. Home renders per request despite `revalidate = 3600` (Medium, medium)
- **Route:** `/`, `/?tab=new`, `/leaders`, `/week`.
- **Observed:** `app/page.tsx` awaits `searchParams`, which opts the page out of ISR. The response sends `no-store`. Chrome's first hit took 1,815 ms TTFB, `/?tab=new` took 804 ms, and warm hits took about 160 ms. `/leaders` and `/week` are `force-dynamic`.
- **Recommendation:** Serve tab and chain as path segments (`/new`, `/trending/base`) so each can be ISR-cached, or read the query client-side over a static shell. `/leaders` can use `revalidate = 3600` since it says "refreshed nightly".

### 9. Phone header, CTA bar and dock lose their blur in Chromium (Medium, small)
- **Route:** all routes at ≤820 px.
- **Observed:** The served CSS (`/_next/static/chunks/35qc7v0ntww89.css`) contains only `-webkit-backdrop-filter:blur(10px)` for `.top`, `.dock` and `.sticky`. The unprefixed `backdrop-filter` was dropped by the build, and computed `backdropFilter` is `none` in Chromium. In the screenshot, the "Why people care" text reads through the dock and the CTA, and Signals text shows behind the logo.
- **Evidence:** `m-390-project-scrolled-sticky.png`.
- **Caveat:** This is a headless Chromium screenshot. Confirm on Android Chrome. Safari should be unaffected.
- **Recommendation:** Make the bar backgrounds opaque (`var(--bg)`), or fix the CSS targets so both declarations survive minification.

### 10. Desktop tables clip their last column at 1280 px (Medium-low, small)
- **Route:** `/`, `/p/[slug]`.
- **Observed:** The Pools table hides "Fee" and part of "Wallets" behind a horizontal scrollbar. Top wallets hides "Last".
- **Evidence:** `03-home-pools-table.jpg`, `06-project-angstrom-why-people-care.jpg`.
- **Recommendation:** Under 1440 px, fold Fee into the pool cell ("MOW / WETH · dynamic") and fold Last into the Trades cell, as the phone layout already does.

### 11. Inter turns "0x" into "0×" (Low, small)
- **Route:** `/`, `/p/[slug]`, `/leaders`.
- **Observed:** "0×0000…0000 / TESTINPROD" and "0×0f48…" appear wherever an address fragment sits in Inter text (pool pairs, unlisted project names). Monospace addresses are fine.
- **Evidence:** `03-home-pools-table.jpg`, `07-leaders-desktop.jpg`.
- **Recommendation:** `font-feature-settings: "calt" 0` on names and pool cells, or wrap address fragments in `.mono`.

### 12. Placeholder-looking pool rows on the #1 project (Low, small)
- **Route:** `/` and `/p/hookrmodularhookv6`.
- **Observed:** "Top 8 of 47 pools by TVL" includes "0×0000…0000 / TESTINPROD", "website / EARN" and several $0 / $0 / – rows. The Top wallets panel shows 9 identical $22.1K, 1-trade, 1/0 rows.
- **Evidence:** `03-home-pools-table.jpg`, `04-home-top-wallets.jpg`.
- **Recommendation:** Hide pools with $0 TVL and $0 24h volume behind "+N inactive pools". The identical wallet rows look like a data artifact and are worth a pipeline check.

### 13. `/week` empty state is bare (Low, small)
- **Observed:** "No digests yet." and then the footer. Nothing says when the first digest arrives or what to do meanwhile.
- **Evidence:** `09-week-empty.jpg`.
- **Recommendation:** "First digest lands Monday, Oct 12. Until then, see Trending or subscribe via RSS."

### 14. `/about` method text predates the indexer and the wash rules (Low, small)
- **Observed:** The method says usage comes from "the Uniswap v4 subgraphs on The Graph". It never mentions the on-chain indexer or the wash rules and red badge, which DESIGN treats as core trust machinery.
- **Recommendation:** Add an "On-chain indexer" paragraph and a "Wash-like volume" paragraph matching DESIGN's package 8 rules.

### 15. Small copy and consistency nits (Low, small)
- "1 trades" in the Top wallets rows on phones (`m-390-project-full-part1.png`): pluralize.
- `/leaders` "Chains covered 10" against 16 chains in the indexer. The label is ambiguous; "Chains with active hooks" would be clearer.
- The header shifts 15 px between pages with and without a scrollbar (`/week` has none; compare `09-week-empty.jpg` with `07-leaders-desktop.jpg`). Fix with `html { scrollbar-gutter: stable; }`.
- Footer links (15 px tall), pool-name links (16 px) and wallet links (18 px) are under the WCAG 2.2 minimum target of 24 px on phones. The dock covers primary nav, so only secondary links are affected. Fix with padding.
- About 30 text nodes under 11 px on home: the "7d" units at 10 px, the axis labels and the table headers at 11 px.

## What's working (for balance)

- **Contrast holds up.** Computed against the real tokens: fg2 #9a9bb2 is 6.8:1 on the panel, fg3 #84859e is 5.2:1, pink2 on the pink tint is 4.9:1, green on the green tint 7.4:1, and red on the red tint 5.5:1. Everything passes AA.
- **Keyboard support is solid.** There is a skip-to-feed link, a 2 px pink focus outline on links and controls, and the search field gets a pink ring.
- **The phone layout is well considered.** Stat tiles move to 2 columns with nothing cut off, the pools table becomes stacked rows, wallet labels wrap, the CTA sticks above a 5-item dock, and there is a "← Trending" back link (`m-390-project.png`).
- **Empty states exist where it counts.** "Daily history starts with the next snapshot." on new projects, and the trades feed has states for unknown pool, no trades and upstream error.
- **Trades API caching works** (60 s browser, 120 s shared). The feed polls only while the panel is on screen and the tab is visible.

## Deliberate choices, judged on usability only

- DESIGN.md (UI direction, 2026-10-07) specifies a single 480 px column and a white light theme. The live site is a dark-only, three-pane terminal with a trade rail, which matches the brief's "trading-terminal feed" direction. I'm not flagging it. DESIGN.md hasn't been updated to record the switch, though, so the doc and the product now disagree.
- The pink-tinted "View pool on Uniswap" for pools that aren't eligible follows the 2026-10-09 three-state routing rule, and the reason line underneath is clear. It reads nearly as strong as a primary action. That's acceptable, but a grey well colour (as the original DESIGN described for non-routable pools) would keep pink reserved for the one real swap action.
